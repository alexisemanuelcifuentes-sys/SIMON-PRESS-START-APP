Is the Level 1 demo really free?

Yes. It includes the complete first level and does not require a purchase. At the end, the game invites you to continue with Premium V3.

What does Premium V3 include?

The complete available adventure: all available levels and worlds, enemies, bosses, story scenes, and the latest Level 1 visual updates.

How do I install it on Windows?

Download the ZIP, extract the complete folder, and run the EXE. Keep the executable and the _internal folder together.

Why can Windows show “Unknown publisher”?

The current independent build is not digitally signed. Download only from Dment Creative's official website, GitHub release, or itch.io page.

Where is the Premium payment processed?

The US$5 purchase and Premium download are handled securely through the official Simon Press Start page on itch.io.

Can I play it on Android?

Not yet. Touch controls, performance, audio, and mobile presentation are still being developed and tested before release.
